How to read this page

Polls come from the public record collated on Wikipedia (snapshot {{ m.polls_date | fmt_day_long if m else '–' }}), each linked there to the original report. Seat margins are the 2022 two-candidate-preferred results from the published pre-election pendulum, with Prahran shown as a Liberal seat after the 2025 by-election. The uniform-swing model is the classic back-of-envelope read, not a forecast: real swings vary seat to seat, sitting members matter, and One Nation on {{ m.average.primary.ONP if m else '–' }}% makes 2022 preference flows a weak guide. Search interest measures attention, not voting intention. Full method.
