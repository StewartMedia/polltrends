How to read this page

Polls come from the public record collated on Wikipedia (snapshot 24 October 2026), each linked there to the original report. Seat margins are the 2022 two-candidate-preferred results from the published pre-election pendulum, with Prahran shown as a Liberal seat after the 2025 by-election. The uniform-swing model is the classic back-of-envelope read, not a forecast: real swings vary seat to seat, sitting members matter, and One Nation on 23.0% makes 2022 preference flows a weak guide. Search interest measures attention, not voting intention. Full method.
