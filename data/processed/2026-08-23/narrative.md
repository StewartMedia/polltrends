One Nation ranked first for Australian political-party search interest in the week from 16 to 22 August, averaging 13.6. Labor ranked second at 2.7, Liberal third at 1.9, and the Greens fourth at 1.0. One Nation peaked at 18 on 17 August and did not fall below 9 during the period.

The other parties recorded much lower and comparatively stable interest. Labor ranged from 2 to 3 and peaked on 17 August. Liberal ranged from 1 to 2 and peaked on 16 August. The Greens remained at 1 throughout the week. No current-week news headlines were available in the snapshot, so the data does not support attributing these movements to a specific event.

Query sentiment was mostly neutral for every party. Liberal had the strongest sentiment score at +0.20, followed by the Greens at +0.16 and One Nation at +0.07. Labor was the only party with a negative overall score, at -0.07, although 37 of its 42 classified queries were neutral.

One Nation's lead follows earlier spikes in the broader series, including a score of 32 on 13 August and 31 on 3 August. Those dates sit outside this reporting week, so they provide context rather than evidence of a new weekly surge.
